## New

- **Maximum Range on ranged weapons.** Every ranged weapon now shows a Maximum Range stat alongside Effective Range. Maximum is the falloff cap; effective is the consistent-hit zone (so a lasgun reaches far past its effective range, while most pistols and rifles cap just a couple meters out).
- **Range augments now apply.** Augments that reduce or extend range (Barrel Extender, Lasgun Harmonizer, and the rest) now move the weapon's Maximum Range instead of doing nothing.

These values are mined straight from the game's own weapon data, matched to each weapon through its in-game loadout.
