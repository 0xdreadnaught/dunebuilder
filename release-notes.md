## Fixed

- **Shield damage now matches the game on more weapons.** The JABAL Spitdart rifles (including Fivefinger's Tripleshot and Blind Fury), both K-28 Lasguns, and the flamethrowers were showing far more shield damage than they really deal. They now read the true value. For example, a grade 0 Regis JABAL Spitdart drops from 121 to 48.
- **Rockets, pyrockets, and explosive-round rifles now show their full damage.** Blast weapons hit for their full value with no body or headshot zone, but the app was trimming them to 40% the way it does an ordinary body shot. Their Damage Per Shot and DPS were understated, and now read the real blast value.
