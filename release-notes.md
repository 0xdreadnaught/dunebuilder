## Fixed

- **Power consumption reductions now apply.** Augments such as Capacity Expander and Lasgun Harmonizer now correctly reduce Power Consumption on energy weapons (lasguns, the Experimental Lightning Gun, Plasma Cannon).
- **Voice Training is a passive, not a technique.** It no longer shows in the techniques grid; it now sits under Skills where it belongs.
- **Field Medicine now counts** toward healkit instant restoration.
- **Clearer cooldown labels.** The Skills panel now distinguishes `Voice Cooldown`, `Voice Registration`, and `Trooper Cooldowns` instead of a single ambiguous "Ability Cooldowns".
