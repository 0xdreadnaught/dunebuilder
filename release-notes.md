## New

- **Skill Tree.** Press `K` to open the full skill tree. Spend points across all five specializations (Bene Gesserit, Mentat, Planetologist, Swordmaster, Trooper). Attribute, ability, and technique nodes respect their in-game prerequisites and point costs.
- **Abilities and Techniques loadout.** From the tree, press `S` to open the abilities and techniques page and drag what you want into your loadout slots.
- **Hydration bar.** A vertical hydration bar now sits beside Health, Stamina, and Power. Click it to set your hydration level. Stamina updates as you cross the dehydrated, hydrated, and fully hydrated thresholds.

## Improved

- **Your skill tree feeds the whole build.** Allocated nodes now flow into the calculations: weapon family damage, headshot and body damage, max health and stamina, power regen, damage mitigation, and more.
- **Short and long blade skills apply correctly.** Short blade and long blade damage nodes now only boost the matching weapons. Dual blades count as short blades.
- **Fuel skills read clearly.** The two Planetologist fuel skills now show as `Fuel Usage (Grd)` and `Fuel Usage (Air)` instead of two identical lines.
- **Leaner save files.** Builds store only your equipment and skill choices. Everything else is computed from those. Hydration is a live toggle and is never saved.
- **Cleaner startup.** The app always opens to a fresh build rather than restoring stale state from a previous session.
